# Brag Plan: my-first-project

## What is it?
A small toolkit that turns an HTML animation into a vertical 1080×1920 MP4 with a natural Kazakh
neural voice (Piper kk_KZ-issai-high), quiet background music, and on-screen words timed to the
moment each one is spoken.

## Angle
"The video that finally speaks Kazakh like a person." The project's own history is the hook: the
first voice was robotic espeak; now it is a neural voice whose words land exactly on screen.
Everything shown is real — the real `ernur.html` player, its real «▶ Дыбыспен ойнату» button,
the real rendered Ernur video with its real soundtrack, and the real word timings from
`video/audio/timings.js`.

## Hook (0–3 s)
Black screen: «Видеоңыз әлі **роботпен** сөйлей ме?» — «роботпен» sits as an unlit tube, then
ignites red with a neon flicker on the first strong hit (1.5 s). Glitch-cut out on the beat.

## Highlights
1. The page (README copy + embedded player): the cursor clicks the real play button — and the
   video starts talking (the click is where the product's own audio takes over).
2. Result + features: the Ernur video keeps playing at the top; four feature cards from the README
   land one per beat-pair: HTML → MP4 · Қазақша нейрон дауыс · Тыныш фондық музыка · Сөз дәл уақытында.
3. Close-up on the strongest feature: camera pushes into the phone while Ernur says
   «Жасанды интеллектпен жұмыс істеуді үйреніп жүрмін»; a timeline under it lights each word at
   its measured time (9.75 с, 10.42 с, …) exactly when it is heard.

## Outro
Starburst logo (the project's mark), «my-first-project», one-line CTA «Өз тік видеоңызды жасаңыз»,
address github.com/aicloudeproduct-lab/-my-first-project typed out.

## User flow worth showing
Open the page → click «▶ Дыбыспен ойнату» → the vertical video plays with Kazakh voice and synced words.

## Tone
- Preset: default (app-store-clean feature cards in the middle)
- Direction: warm, confident product film; one hard, red, glitchy hook, then calm and clean.

## Format / duration
vertical 1080×1920, 30 fps, 23 s
